# Productivity Is About Making Space for What Matters

Productivity is not about filling every hour with work. It is about choosing the work that moves you forward and protecting time to do it well.

## Start with one clear priority

At the beginning of the day, choose the one task that would make the day feel meaningful if it were complete. Finish that before getting pulled into messages, notifications, or smaller tasks.

## Work in focused blocks

Try a short, distraction-free block of time:

1. Decide exactly what you will work on.
2. Silence notifications and close unrelated tabs.
3. Work until the block ends.
4. Take a real break before starting again.

Small, consistent blocks are easier to sustain than waiting for a perfect long session.

## Keep your system simple

A notebook, a notes app, or a small task list is enough. The best system is the one you can review every day without creating extra work for yourself.

Progress comes from returning to the important work again and again.
